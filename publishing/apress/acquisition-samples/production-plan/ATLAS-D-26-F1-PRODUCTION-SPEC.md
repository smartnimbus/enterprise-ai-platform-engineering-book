## Exact frozen Default (paste verbatim)

> Default to tenant, use-case and trajectory-level showback based on cost per successful task, with budgets and invoice reconciliation.

## Full F1 content source — keep the canonical meaning

**Context.** Multi-tenant model and tool charges must be allocated to outcomes and owners.

**Why.** Token charges omit retries, tool calls, compute and shared overhead. Finance needs visibility into failed tasks as well as successful ones.

**Trade-off.** This buys unit-economic accountability at the cost of attribution engineering.

**When to deviate.** Small pilot uses temporary showback only; Regulated contract prescribes a fixed-cost recovery model. Render as separate conditions in the final one-page layout; these are exceptions, not overriding defaults.

**Reversibility.** Two-way door.

**Artefact.** `cost-allocation-policy.xlsx`.

## Required evidence / reproducible reader deliverable

- Primary decision record: `cost-allocation-policy.xlsx` with option selected, owner, approver, verification criterion, evidence pointer and conditions for re-evaluation.
- Reader-facing worked illustration: Hypothetical showback calculation separating successful tasks, failed tasks, model use, tool calls and shared overhead; numbers clearly illustrative, not observed Meridian spend.
- Layout or illustration: Contribution-of-cost waterfall with numerator/denominator: total task costs ÷ verified successful tasks; use hypothetical units.
- Citation discipline: exact Atlas source is normative; Mermaid/Meridian evidence is synthetic; any cost or adoption statistics must carry separate, dated references.
- Sample artefact must show required fields and the limits of what the worked illustration proves. Do not manufacture a signed token, approved payout, source-precedence rule, production egress test, paid unit-cost outcome or completed retirement.
