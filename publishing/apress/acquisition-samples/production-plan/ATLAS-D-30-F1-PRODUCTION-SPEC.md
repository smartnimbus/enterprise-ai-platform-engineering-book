## Exact frozen Default (paste verbatim)

> Default to a formal retirement gate proving token revocation, data disposition, preserved obligations and tested export or migration.

## Full F1 content source — keep the canonical meaning

**Context.** Stopping execution leaves credentials, memory, evidence, vendor contracts and legal obligations behind.

**Why.** The identity, context and telemetry stores do not share one deletion command. Each owner must record disposal, archival or legal hold against a common inventory.

**Trade-off.** This buys safe closure and portability at the cost of exit rehearsals and retention administration.

**When to deviate.** Legal hold prevents a specified deletion; Phased migration requires dual running; Decommission is delayed by unresolved data ownership. Render as separate conditions in the final one-page layout; these are exceptions, not overriding defaults.

**Reversibility.** One-way door.

**Artefact.** `retirement-certificate-and-exit-plan.md`.
