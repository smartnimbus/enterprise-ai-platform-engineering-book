### Required argument and learning objectives

A context input can be relevant but unauthorised, and authorised records can still conflict. The reader must identify the C Structural Layer's contract, recheck access before consumption, preserve canonical IDs/source provenance/effective time, leave a conflict unresolved without a precedence rule, prevent unapproved durable-memory assertion, distinguish working context/memory/audit evidence, and trace D-11–D-14 to specific artefacts. Context does **not** issue Gateway execution permission, declare retail truth or act as an additional Control Plane.
