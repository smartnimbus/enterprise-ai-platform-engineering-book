### What to measure, and what not to overclaim
| Measure | Useful interpretation | How to collect without invention |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Qualified reader conversations | Do readers recognise and need the decision framework? | Dated interview notes with respondent roles and consent |
| Artefact application rate | Could readers complete decisions without a lecture? | Workshop pre/post completed decision records, anonymised |
| Independent technical corrections | Does the method survive expert challenge? | Named reviewers, review date, issue log, author response |
| Conference acceptance and repeat invitation | Is the topic compelling to independent organisers? | Public agenda/speaker page; do not count unaccepted CFPs |
| Newsletter opt-in and returning readers | Is there durable demand for more? | Transparent, consent-based subscriber reports |
| Publisher response | Is the package commercially credible? | Submission tracker, request type, requested follow-up |
| Downloads to real use | Do templates move from viewing to practice? | Optional feedback survey, not invasive tracking |
| Commercial training demand | Would professionals allocate time or budget? | Confirmed trial bookings, paid orders or explicit requests |


**Authority claim threshold:** Until independently verified projects or review history are available, use “developed a reference decision method based on published and synthetic cases,” not “industry-proven framework,” “deployed at twelve firms,” or “regulator-approved.” Names and credentials should be backed by documents and permission to disclose.

## 8. Editor questions likely to determine progression

1. What exactly has the author **personally** designed, operated, and taught that equips them to write a definitive enterprise reference? Please substantiate without disclosing clients without permission.
2. How is the reader's use of D-10/D-14 measurably different from the material in *Governance-First AI* and *Building AI Agent Platforms*?
3. Can I see both completed sample chapters and six properly typeset F1 decision pages?
4. Does every one of the 58 chapters deliver a distinct reader outcome, and is the 478-page plan technically plausible?
5. How many verified figures, permission-sensitive extracts, third-party citations and nonhuman drafting disclosures will production need?
6. Who is writing, reviewing and illustrating the remaining chapters, with what actual capacity and delivery dates?
7. What independent reader feedback shows the material is teachable rather than an internally coherent architectural taxonomy?
