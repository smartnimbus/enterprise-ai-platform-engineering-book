## Six-month scenario, scheduling and staffing

Frozen TOC allocates 448 chapter pages (about 478 total in proposal). This scenario budgets 2.1 human hours/page on high-risk/technical chapters and 1.55 hours/page elsewhere, including drafting and technical review; subtracts limited reuse of existing Chapter 16 draft; adds 22% rework/coordination and 75 integration/figure/rights hours. **Scenario: ~1,090 combined human hours** (not measured velocity). 26 weeks require **~42 combined productive human hours/week**. One architect at 15 hours/week offers **390 hours** and cannot meet that six-month full-manuscript target without drastically smaller publisher-authorised scope or additional people.

Suggested capacity: accountable lead author/architect 15 hours/week; technical writer/co-author 20 hours/week; independent specialist reviewer/editor 10 hours/week = **45 effective hours/week**, assuming review queues and scheduling work. These are distinct roles; AI-generated drafts do not eliminate human review. A 15–25% schedule reserve is advisable because trial metrics do not yet exist; the estimate already includes a 22% activity buffer but not publisher external decision time.

## Six-month decision

A **proposal submission** within 4–6 weeks is plausible with verified human author facts and reviewed samples. A **complete 58-chapter manuscript delivered within six months** is a stretch commitment requiring approximately 42 productive combined human hours/week, an effective editorial lead and qualified reviewers, reliable source access and a successful pilot. At 15 hours/week solo, no credible six-month full-manuscript promise can be made; a 12–24-month or team-funded schedule is safer, subject to observed drafting/review velocity.

## Manuscript delivery and production

The fixed 58-chapter plan allows work to proceed in coordinated groups once the technical exemplars establish the voice and depth expected of the book. The present material includes a substantive Chapter 16 draft, extensive Chapter 13 and Chapter 16 authoring blueprints, the full Decision Atlas catalogue, the Meridian synthetic evidence fixture and detailed chapter briefs. These reduce planning and assembly effort but do not replace original narrative authorship or external technical review.

The current planning estimate for a complete manuscript is approximately **980 qualified human hours** including chapter writing, reuse, technical editing, citation work and integration. The estimated extent is 448 chapter pages plus front matter and index. A six-month delivery would require sustained capacity of approximately 38 qualified human hours a week across authorship, specialist review and editorial work. The actual contractual date should be agreed only after the author and any collaborators confirm their available capacity and the scope of the publisher's requested sample and figure work.

Apress can first receive two fully edited technical examples—Chapter 13 (*Context*) and Chapter 16 (*Gateway Control Plane*)—together with six representative F1 decision pages. The remaining manuscript proceeds through the fixed structure in a phased editorial sequence; chapter order and frozen technical meanings remain unchanged.
