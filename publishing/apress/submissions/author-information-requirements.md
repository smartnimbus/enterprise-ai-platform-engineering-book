# Author information and rights completion worksheet

Status: NOT SUPPLIED unless stated. Complete privately with author of record; do not commit personal contact data, private CV or signatures to public GitHub. This worksheet is not a declaration.

| Required input | Completion requirement |
|---|---|
| Publishing name and contact | Author-confirmed legal/publishing name, email and contact details |
| Professional title and organisation | Authorised wording, actual experience |
| Biography and CV | 120–180-word attributable biography; two-page CV with verified roles, dates and relevant outputs |
| Audience and promotion | Actual channels and commitments, no invented audience metrics |
| Author/contributor roles | Names and contribution declarations; consent |
| Rights and originality | Confirm ownership/licences, quotes, diagrams and third-party materials; record permissions |
| AI use | Factual account of tools and extent of drafting, editing, illustration and validation; author accountability; check applicable publisher policy |
| Reviewer records | Actual names/roles/dates, exact accepted revisions and corrections; privately where required |
| Delivery capacity | Named productive hours/week, reviewer availability, delivery date agreed with publisher; estimate not commitment |
| Official form | Complete current publisher form with verified fields; confirm Apress commissioning contact and requested file formats |
| Final author approval | Explicit approval of exact package and transmission |
