## Editor questions: revised answers and evidence needed

**Why is another agent-platform book required?** The acquisition pitch and proposal explain the decision-to-evidence method, where an enterprise architect must defend funding, ownership, action authority, negative verification, cost and exit. **Evidence still needed:** completed Context and Gateway chapters showing the method with readable mechanisms and worked records.

**What distinguishes it from *Building AI Agent Platforms*?** That book offers substantive platform engineering, product and operational material. This proposal is a decision-oriented reference centred on accountable choice and its evidence, not a competing claim to the whole platform-building territory. **Evidence still needed:** six consistent F1 decision pages and comparative sample reading.

**Who is the author and why should we trust them?** The manuscript is written from an enterprise architecture perspective with practical decision structures. **Evidence still needed:** verified CV, attributable experience, public contributions and any authorised client examples. No biography has been invented.

**Can a 58-chapter manuscript be delivered?** The frozen TOC allocates 448 chapter pages. The production scenario estimates about 980 human hours of remaining work and requires a team-level allocation of about 38 qualified hours per week for six months. **Evidence still needed:** named, committed capacity, reviewer schedule and an agreed manuscript delivery date.

**Is Meridian an actual deployed example?** No. It is a transparent fictional case and synthetic evidence fixture illustrating consistent claims and negative outcomes. **Evidence still needed:** completed scene-to-record captions and any separately sourced field examples; do not imply production attestation.

**Are the diagrams and materials cleared?** Original source diagrams can be created from book-owned models, but figures and historical research may have third-party restrictions. **Evidence still needed:** a permissions ledger for all included illustrations and quoted external materials.

**Will the book remain useful after model APIs change?** Its durable teaching units are option appraisal, ownership boundaries, authorisation semantics, release tests, cost and retirement. Volatile protocols or products will have dated references. **Evidence still needed:** final, dated technical bibliography for protocol-specific pages.

**Why Apress?** This is an applied professional technology reference for working architects and platform leaders, complementary to Apress titles in enterprise agent adoption, AI governance and identity. **Evidence still needed:** genuine editorial assessment of scope, format and commercial case.
