## Study protocol
Question: can independent practitioners use the frozen chapter and templates to make defensible investment decisions without author assistance? Three synthetic, standalone case pairs with controlled evidence updates; exact cases, outcomes and worked keys restricted until data collection closes. The participant, not a case or rating, is the primary unit. Target 24 eligible starters (four each enterprise architect, solution architect, CTO/executive, platform lead, FinOps/Finance specialist, security/governance architect); balanced exploratory pilot 12 (two each). Require relevant practical responsibility, no chapter authorship or case exposure; disclose prior familiarity and conflicts, separate substantially exposed cohort. Voluntary consent, fixed compensation if used, no confidential employer data. Separate identity mapping, individual responses and quote permissions; private controlled custody; aggregate reporting only.
Independent facilitator, two independent assessors, independent adjudicator; no author coaching or scoring. Six case orders, four participants each, role-balanced slots. Offline frozen chapter, two figures, four blank templates, case-specific evidence, calculator/blank spreadsheet/editors only. No other sources, collaboration or AI. Record allowed accessibility accommodations, assistance, deviations and active time. Administration/baseline 15 min; reading 30; three initial exercises 75 min each; three updates 20 min each; post questionnaire 15; total 345 min, plus breaks. Lock and hash initial records before updates; retain history. Initial Business Case, Option ADR, Benefits Register, Funding Decision Record and workings; updated FD plus linked changes to BC/ADR/BR. No participant is told the outcome distribution; no actual investment or deployment. No release of future cases or keys.
Pre-register protocol version/hash, sample/stopping rule, inclusion, time and accommodation rules, rubric, endpoints, assessors/calibration, privacy and data lock before execution. Do not amend after observing data; record deviations or a separate study version. Score both phases against the evidence available at each phase. Do not reward preference for shared architecture.

## Scoring and analysis
Ten domains, integer 0–4 with criterion anchors: funded demand 10, control equivalence 12, sourcing 10, lifecycle cost/attribution 14, uncertainty/benefits 8, ownership 8, four authorisations 10, Decision-to-Evidence 12, funding disposition 10, reconsideration/custody 6. Score=sum(weight*rating/4). Six non-compensable material gates: unsupported demand/equivalence used for approval; mandatory control bypass/unsupplied technical pass; material financial error supporting decision; authority transfer; unsupported commitment/approval; unsupported continuation or altered/fabricated history. Separate material assistance/protocol breach invalidates independent endpoint. Case pass >=80 AND both scoped dispositions defensible AND four r1 records and linked r2 amendments complete AND no critical gate AND independent valid completion. Person pass all three. Record canonical match separately; alternative label only with evidence-supported same-scope rationale and adjudication. No post hoc key changes. Assessors score blinded submissions separately; retain raw ratings; adjudicate disposition, defensibility, completeness, independence, critical, pass disagreements, score gaps >10 or domain gaps >=2; independent third assessor resolves unresolved. Author excluded.
All eligible starters with retained consent in denominator, incomplete = non-pass, withdrawals handled per consent and disclosed as permissible aggregate. Per-case rates with same denominator; report rated denominators for scores. Wilson 95% intervals as descriptive precision; 72 case pairs are repeated within 24 people, not independent observations. Role/order descriptive, no causal learning or population claim; no control arm. Pre-adjudication raw label, pass and critical agreement, nominal kappa when estimable, score differences and adjudication counts. Separate qualitative usability from actual performance.
Editorial evidence targets at N=24 (not publisher rules or probabilities): >=20 all-three, >=21 each case, >=22 complete within assigned windows, median participant mean >=85, each case/domain median >=3, zero G2/G4 in rated pairs, no recurrent critical pattern (same gate >=3 people or >=3 in one case), >=85% raw pass and phase label agreement with complete paired coverage, resolved adjudication. Stronger >=22 all-three, >=23 each, >=23 complete, median >=90, zero critical flags, >=90% pass and >=85% label agreement; retain all base requirements. Pilot exploratory. Report actual counts, intervals, missingness, adverse results, deviations, anonymised consent-permitted artefacts, limitations, independence; no invented endorsement or acquisition probability.
