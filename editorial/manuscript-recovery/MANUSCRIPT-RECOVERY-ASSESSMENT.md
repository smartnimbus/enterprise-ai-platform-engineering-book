## Recovery methodology and limitations

The complete GitHub `main` tree was inspected and the exact TOC, chapter blueprint, Chapter 16 draft and Phase 4/production registers were reopened. The chapter recovery matrix is computed from the 58 named TOC records and each matching blueprint's `### Chapter N` section; actual source files are linked in the input register. Existing complete diagrams and final citation bibliographies have **not** been verified chapter by chapter. A diagram or table mentioned in a brief is classified as a candidate, never a completed figure. All numerical ingredient-recovery scores are constrained editorial planning estimates, not content-similarity measurements or independently readied pages.

## Production strategy

**Pilots:** Chapter 16 substantive edit; Chapter 13 full draft; Chapter 23 F1 production pages. Then Chapter 10 overview and 11–22 owner-specific explanations, Chapters 1–9 reader argument, Chapters 24–34 stage applications, Chapters 35–40 operations, Chapters 41–50 playbooks and Chapters 51–58 references, followed by whole-manuscript editorial integration. The *book chapter order stays frozen*; the drafting order does not.

**Fastest useful publisher sample set:** Chapter 1 (reader promise and accessible opening), Chapter 13 (deep structural walkthrough), Chapter 16 (Gateway, decision and evidence). Submit Chapter 23/Atlas as a separate three-page excerpt D-10/D-14/D-18 only after the missing D-14 F1 page is authored. For reviewer stress-testing prefer 13,16 and 23 together; for acquisition readability lead with 1,13,16.

**Do not factory-generate:** normative decision defaults; claims about deployment, cryptographic proof or successful financial mutation; author approvals; unlicensed market data; high-risk identity, security and non-bypass assertions without appropriate specialist review. Human authors retain the final editorial voice and domain explanation.
