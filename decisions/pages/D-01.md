### D-01: Adopt a shared Fabric

**Context.** Multiple use cases are requesting overlapping tool, identity and verification controls, but a shared service creates recurring operational obligations.

**Default.** Default to domain-local delivery until at least two funded production use cases need the same control contracts and a positive shared-cost case is evidenced.

**Why.** A shared Fabric introduces support cost before it creates benefit. Count duplicated controls and onboarding time, then compare their costs with shared operating overhead. In a hypothetical annual comparison of two funded production use cases needing the same controls, domain-local costs GBP 60,000 + GBP 60,000; shared costs GBP 70,000 + GBP 30,000. The illustrative GBP 20,000 difference is a case to verify, not earned savings or an investment approval.

**Trade-off.** This buys coherent controls and reuse at the cost of platform operations.

**When to deviate.**
- One isolated use case.
- Hard contractual segregation.
- No confirmed shared-control demand.

**Reversibility.** Two-way door.

**Artefact.** `fabric-adoption-adr.md`.

*Illustration - hypothetical annual comparison.*
- Domain-local: 60,000 + 60,000 = GBP 120,000
- Shared: 70,000 service + 30,000 onboarding = GBP 100,000
- Difference: GBP 20,000; validate like-for-like scope and costs.
- Option: evaluate shared | Owner / approver: to be assigned
- Verify: funded demand and positive cost case | Evidence: comparison
- Re-evaluate: demand, segregation, or cost changes.
