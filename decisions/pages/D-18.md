### D-18: Prove Gateway non-bypassability

**Context.** A gateway control plane is ineffective if tools can call external targets directly.

**Default.** Default to mandatory PEP checks on mutable external actions, enforced by credentials, routing policy and egress controls.

**Why.** Architecture diagrams are insufficient proof. A denial and direct-bypass negative test must show that unapproved side effects cannot succeed. In synthetic Meridian, MF-EVD-DN-001 records Gateway denial, then MF-EVT-013 separately tests the direct service-to-ledger route without the PEP. Egress blocks that simulated route; this is not evidence of a deployed firewall or every possible bypass.

**Trade-off.** This buys enforceable admission at the cost of proxy dependencies and outage planning.

**When to deviate.**
- Restricted offline worker cannot reach external systems.
- An explicitly approved trusted path implements equivalent PEP controls.

**Reversibility.** One-way door.

**Artefact.** `gateway-bypass-proof.md`.

- Direct route: MF-EVT-013 | DENIED_BY_NETWORK_EGRESS

- Option: mandatory PEP | Owner / approver: assign for deployment
- Verify: normal denial and blocked direct route | Evidence: receipt + event
- Re-evaluate: credential, routing, or egress change.
