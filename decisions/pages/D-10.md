### D-10: Authorise mutable tool actions

**Context.** A generated tool intent is not permission to change an external system.

**Default.** Default to Gateway-enforced action authorisation with a signed, scoped receipt and idempotency key before every external mutation.

**Why.** The policy enforcement point (PEP) checks tenant, actor chain, resource scope, risk, and approval. Tooling executes only after successful validation and logs the resulting side effect. In synthetic Meridian, MF-EVD-DG-001 records effective refund:request, not refund:execute; MF-EVD-AU-001 records DENY for MF-ACT-REF-001. MF-EVD-DN-001 records no external call and zero financial mutations.

**Trade-off.** This buys defensible action boundaries at the cost of policy-check latency.

**When to deviate.**
- A read-only call has an approved reduced policy path.
- Bulk action uses a separately authorised bounded batch.
- Emergency operations require recorded break-glass review.

**Reversibility.** Two-way door.

**Artefact.** `action-authorisation-contract.json`.

*Illustration - synthetic decision and enforcement.*
- Request: MF-REQ-REF-001 | Delegation: MF-EVD-DG-001
- Decision: MF-EVD-AU-001 = DENY | Receipt: MF-EVD-DN-001
- authorised_execution_token = null
- signed_authorisation_status = NOT_ISSUED_DENIED
- external_tool_called = false | financial_mutation_count = 0
- Option: deny | Owner / approver: record in real contract
- Verify: no mutation | Evidence: receipts | Recheck: scope or policy change.
