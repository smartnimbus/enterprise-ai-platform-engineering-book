### D-14: Reconcile semantic identities

**Context.** Different sources may name the same entity or disagree on its status.

**Default.** Default to canonical IDs with source lineage and explicit unresolved-conflict states; never silently choose the newest record as truth.

**Why.** Context owns the entity match and conflict record; the domain data owner defines precedence. In synthetic Meridian, MF-REC-A-001 reports ACTIVE and MF-REC-B-001 reports CLOSED for MF-PROD-0091, both effective 2027-02-22 at 00:00 UTC. MF-EVD-RC-001 records UNRESOLVED and escalation to the Retail Data Steward; a later recording time does not establish authority. Verification tests whether a blocked conflict leads to a side effect.

**Trade-off.** This buys consistent meaning at the cost of stewardship and escalations.

**When to deviate.**
- Authoritative source has published precedence rules.
- A disposable unlinked context item needs no canonical entity.

**Reversibility.** Two-way door.

**Artefact.** `semantic-reconciliation-record.json`.

*Illustration - synthetic claim comparison.*
- A: ACTIVE at 09:00 | B: CLOSED at 09:10 (recorded UTC)
- Common effective time: 2027-02-22 00:00 UTC
- MF-EVD-RC-001: UNRESOLVED | MF-TASK-STEWARDSHIP-001
- Option: retain conflict | Owner: MF-HUM-RET-OWN-001
- Approver: none recorded | Verify: no unsafe side effect
- Evidence: reconciliation record | Recheck: published precedence or new evidence.
