### D-26: Allocate cost and budgets

**Context.** Multi-tenant model and tool charges must be allocated to outcomes and owners.

**Default.** Default to tenant, use-case and trajectory-level showback based on cost per successful task, with budgets and invoice reconciliation.

**Why.** Token charges omit retries, tool calls, compute, and shared overhead. Include failed-task costs in the numerator; count only verified successful tasks in the denominator. In a hypothetical batch of 100 tasks, 80 succeed and 20 fail; GBP 160 total divided by 80 verified successes yields GBP 2 per successful task. Reconcile allocated costs to invoices before using showback to govern budgets.

**Trade-off.** This buys unit-economic accountability at the cost of attribution engineering.

**When to deviate.**
- Small pilot uses temporary showback only.
- Regulated contract prescribes a fixed-cost recovery model.

**Reversibility.** Two-way door.

**Artefact.** `cost-allocation-policy.xlsx`.

*Illustration - hypothetical cost contribution (GBP).*
- Model 70 + tools 30 + compute 20 + overhead 40 = 160
- Same total by outcome: successful 120 + failed 40 = 160
- 160 / 80 verified successes = GBP 2 per successful task
- Option: showback | Owner / approver: to be assigned
- Verify: allocation totals reconcile | Evidence: invoice and outcome records
- Re-evaluate: tariff, allocation, budget, or success-rate change.
