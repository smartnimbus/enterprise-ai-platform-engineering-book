# Decision Atlas Template v1.0

**Authority:** Style Bible v1.2, F1. This is a transcription of the governing template, not a replacement.

```markdown
### D-XX: [Decision name]

**Context.** One paragraph. What problem does this decision solve?
**Default.** One sentence. The recommended position.
**Why.** Two to four sentences. The evidence, the principle, or the measured pattern.
**Trade-off.** One sentence in the form "This buys X at the cost of Y."
**When to deviate.** Two to four bullets. The conditions under which the default is wrong.
**Reversibility.** One-way door / Two-way door.
**Artefact.** The document, record, or configuration this decision produces.
**See also.** Cross-references.
```

**Mandatory production controls**

- Exactly these eight fields, in this order, and the original decision ID/title. One typeset page maximum; no additional fields, narrative or decorative sidebar.
- Retain the frozen default, trade-off, reversibility, deviation conditions and artefact; add grounded case evidence within the existing **Why** field only. Do not reinterpret any frozen choice.
- Keep the **Why** field to two to four sentences total, including the example. Do not mistake simulation for production verification.
- Part IV uses prescriptive voice, no narrative, no contractions, British spelling and the controlled vocabulary.
- Use stable, cross-checked evidence IDs; output artefact remains the original filename, not the specimen receipt.
- Pagination is verified in the final trim/typography. Source Sans 3 and Source Serif 4 are mandated at production; these preview PDFs use substitute fonts only because the specified fonts are not present in this sandbox.
- Acceptance: eight headings, 2–4 deviation bullets, unmodified defaults/artefacts, provenance validation, independent usability, one-page fit, and no invented production evidence.
